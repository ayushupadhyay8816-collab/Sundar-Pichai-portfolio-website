01 / THE PERSON

About Sundar Pichai

“Great things are often built by teams that stay curious, keep learning and think about the people they serve.”

A technology executive known for product thinking, calm leadership and a global outlook.

Sundar Pichai, born Pichai Sundararajan on June 10, 1972, in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India, is an Indian-American business executive and the chief executive officer of Google and its parent company, Alphabet. He grew up in Chennai and developed an early interest in technology and engineering. After studying metallurgical engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, he moved to the United States for further education.

He earned a master’s degree in materials science and engineering from Stanford University and an MBA from the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania. Pichai joined Google in 2004 and worked on products including Google Toolbar and the Chrome web browser. He later led product and engineering teams across major Google services. He became Google’s CEO in 2015 and took on the role of Alphabet’s CEO in 2019. His leadership has included a strong focus on artificial intelligence, helpful digital products, cloud services and making technology accessible to people around the world. His career is often cited as an example of lifelong learning, teamwork and steady leadership.
